Where film craft meets what’s next

FILMAVATION is the production studio I started in Orlando in 2022. The name is the mission: film plus innovation. We pair classic cinema craft with the newest tools on set, like LED-wall virtual production, and we’re building toward AI in pre-production next. As a student at UF’s Innovation Academy with a minor in Innovation (AI), I’m always testing what new tech can do for a shoot.
