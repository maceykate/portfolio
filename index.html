About

Thrown into the deep end

One of my favorite sayings is to throw yourself into the deep end, and that’s exactly how I started: learning production at an underwater camera rental house, prepping housings and cinema cameras for shoots where every seal and setting has to be right before the gear goes in the water.

That same hands-on approach runs through FILMAVATION Studios, which I started in 2022. Since then we’ve just hit 200 paid projects, produced, shot and edited for clients like Morgan & Morgan, the Orlando Magic and Rolling Loud, with work featured in The New York Times, The Washington Post, Teen Vogue and CNN.

On bigger sets, I've been a camera PA on Netflix's UNTOLD, built camera packages for Disneyland's 70th anniversary, and spent summer 2026 on Peacock projects at NBCUniversal in New York. My favorite part of production is the part most people never see: the gear prep, the schedule juggling and the workflow that keeps the shoot day calm.

Off set, it's film photography, live music and more stand-up than I'll admit.
